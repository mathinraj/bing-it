chrome://newtab

The Bing auto searcher that types like a person.

Pick a number and press go. It types, reads and takes breaks in a background tab, using today's Google Trends.

Add to Chrome

Free. Chrome and Microsoft Edge.

Then get back to your day.

It opens its own tab in the background and works there. The badge on the toolbar counts the searches, so you never have to look.

Watch it type.

One key at a time. 45 to 185 ms per key, a little longer after spaces, and now and then a pause to think.

Demo session. Your scroll drives the clock, the timing rules are the extension's own.

Then it reads.

With scrolling on, it works down the results in uneven jumps, stops to read, and sometimes scrolls back up.

It waits like a person waits.

A fixed or random delay between searches. The floating timer on the Bing tab always shows what happens next.

And takes coffee breaks.

Turn them on and every few searches it steps away for a few minutes. Every 5 searches, for 3 to 6 minutes, unless you say otherwise.

Tune how human it looks.

These are the settings from the options page, working. Change any of them and the forecast redoes the math.

Real searches, not a canned list.

Trending searches for your region go first, then a shuffled pool. No two sessions get the same queue.

Your turn.

Pick a number in the popup and add it to Chrome. It installs in Microsoft Edge from the same store.
